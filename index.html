What’s inside

Free to use, on the web and on Android.

  • Progress dashboard

    Live progress for the year, quarter, month and week, with a quote of the day.

  • Goals

    Set daily, weekly, monthly and yearly goals, and tick them off on the habit sheet.

  • Life Calendar

    See your whole year at a glance and plan around it.

  • Notepad and Docs

    Rich text notes with highlighting. Notes you share with a team stay in sync both ways.

  • Team goals

    Create or join a team with an invite code. Share goals, chat and cheer each other on.

  • Inspire feed

    Stories and songs matched to your goals. Paste any YouTube link to keep your own.

  • Guest or account

    Start as a guest right away, or sign in with email to sync your data. Light and dark mode included.
